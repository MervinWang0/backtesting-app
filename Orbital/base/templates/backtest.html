A Breakout Strategy buys/sells when a stock price moves beyond a defined support/resistance level. We use the donchian channels method of determining support and resistance levels.

The upper band is considered the resistance line and the lower band the support line. A breakout occurs when the price for a particular day is outside of the support or resistance lines.

A trading signal is generated in the case of a breakout.

This strategy is relatively similar to Moving Average Crossover, except that it takes in a short, medium, and long window, and uses an exponential moving average to calculate lines instead of a simple moving average. Which gives greater weight to recent prices.

The MACD Line and Signal Line are computed from the input parameters and used to determine the orders.

A Mean Reversion Strategy seeks to identify when an asset is overbought/oversold and generate sell/buy signals respectively. This implementation of a Mean Reversion Strategy makes use of there indicators, Z-Score, Bollinger Bands, and RSI

A MAJORITY logic makes more trades than a AND logic, but is comparatively riskier

There are multiple different Momentum trading strategies, but this specific one uses three indicators and is thus called Triple indicator Momentum Trading. The three indicators are MACD lines, rising strength index(RSI) and an exponential moving average.

A Momentum trading strategy seeks to identify the trend and ride the trend. It encapsulates the phrase "Buy High, Sell Higher".

A Moving Average Crossover is one of the simplest algorithmic trading strategies. It calculates two simple moving averages for any given day. Using a short and a long window. When these lines cross each other, it signifies that a trade should be executed.

In fact, this it is widely considered to be the most famous trading strategy, basically the equivalent of "Hello World" for algorithmic trading.

The Rate of Change indicator uses a rate of change(ROC) value. Of which positive ROC indicates upwards price acceleration and negative ROC means downwards price acceleration.

There are multiple ways of generating a signal based on the ROC value, in some cases, the ROC value is used as a predictor as to the market condition, but in this case, we are using it as it crosses the zero line to generate signals

The Stochastic oscillator is used here as a mean reversion strategy, based on a window, it calculates two lines, a k line and d line. Which is then used to determine the market condition.

Fun Fact: It was created in 1950s by Dr George C. Lane. Who described it with the quote "Before a rocket can turn down, it must slow down. Momentum always changes direction before price."
