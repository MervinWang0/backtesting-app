A Monte Carlo Simulation is a way of testing the robustness of a trading strategy. Basically, it asks the question, "This strategy trades well for this situation, but would it trade well in an alternate situation?"

First it generates plausible random versions of the original price history of the asset. Then it performs the backtesting strategy on the randomized data.

Afterwards the results are represented as a distribution. Representing the likelihood of the strategy performing well in different situations.
